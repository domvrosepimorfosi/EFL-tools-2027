Be the Bot

Two roles, one lesson question, many AI-style answers. 🎨🤖

(Developed from an idea by Matt Miller in Ditch That Textbook)

🧑‍🎓 Human: asks a question about today’s lesson and types it into the box.

🤖 Bot: answers like an AI chatbot. Use clear English and important words from the lesson.

Work in pairs or with your teacher. When you finish, save your work on the Class Board so everyone can see different “bot” answers.

Step 1 · Human

🧑‍🎓 Ask your question

Human: type a simple question about the lesson. Make it clear and easy to understand for an AI (and your classmates!).

Your question can be about grammar, vocabulary, reading texts, history, science – anything from today’s lesson.

Step 2 · Bot

🤖 Be the bot

Bot: answer like an AI chatbot. Use good English, full sentences and key words from the lesson. Imagine you are an AI helper!

Think: how would a good AI teacher explain this idea to one of your classmates?
