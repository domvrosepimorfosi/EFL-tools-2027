🚀 QUICK STARTS FOR THE DAY 4
Send Students on a Search Inquiry
Ask a question connected to your lesson and challenge students to find the answer using their browser. Craft prompts that require more than a copy‑and‑paste—questions that make them think about how they’re searching. Debrief how they searched and how they judged source reliability.
Cultivate Creativity With AI Images Padlet
In a Padlet board, have students generate and post an AI image about today’s topic—a vocabulary visual or an image that captures a mood. Classmates add captions to each other’s images.
Hook Curiosity With a Custom Song Suno
Create an AI‑generated tune that hints at today’s concept, question, or theme. Play it as students enter and ask them to listen for keywords or clues. Make it ahead of time—or create it live for a wow moment.
Check In with Emojis Emoji
Have students open a Google Doc/Slide/Note and choose an emoji that matches their feelings, confidence about yesterday’s topic, or a prediction for today. You get instant feedback at a glance.

✅ CHECKS FOR UNDERSTANDING 4
Make a Mystery Mic Drop Moment Padlet Voice
Students type a one‑sentence summary into Padlet’s AI Voice tool. Turn on “Hide Author” for anonymity, then let classmates listen and vote with the five‑star reaction setting.
Spot the Lie 2 Truths & a Lie
Students post two true statements and one false one about the learning so far on a shared Padlet. Classmates like the post they think is the lie before authors reveal answers.
Plot a Timeline in Ten Padlet Timeline
Create a timeline board and have small groups map key events—history moments, process steps, or story scenes. Compare timelines between groups and discuss differences.
Drive Deeper Discussion Wayground
Use Wayground Presentations (formerly Quizizz) to upload slides and insert polls or word clouds. Responses appear instantly. Switch to a collaborative whiteboard in one click to extend discussion.

💬 COLLABORATION & DISCUSSION 4
Co‑Write Creative Stories Padlet
Use a Padlet set to columns. Post the opening and let students continue the story via comments or new posts. Add AI images for illustrations. You’ll end with a class‑built story.
Take Collaboration to the Next Level Canva Whiteboard
In Canva’s digital whiteboard, groups co‑create visuals like character maps, concept webs, or brainstorms. Tap the library of elements and fonts for expressive work.
Build a Collaborative Graffiti Wall Padlet
Start with a Padlet sandbox template as a graffiti wall for ideas, questions, and visuals. Use it as a warm‑up, a mid‑lesson check, or a closing reflection.
Create a Top 5 (or Flop 5) List Ranking
Students post top‑five lists (e.g., events in a story, ways to solve a problem, or biggest mistakes by a historical figure). Turn on voting so the class can like favorites.

✍️ WRITING & CREATION 6
Boost Student Brainstorming Visually Freeform
On iPad/Mac, Freeform needs no setup—just open and create. Students build sketchnotes or visual study guides with drawing tools, sticky notes, and shapes.
Animate Student Thinking Keynote
Use Keynote’s Line Draw to animate sketches that reveal thinking step‑by‑step—e.g., the water cycle, photosynthesis, or story plot development.
Expand Student Planning Google Draw
Google Draw offers a simple shared canvas for digital storyboards, mind maps, flowcharts, experiment steps, or novel event maps—editable in real time.
Provide Individualized Writing Feedback MagicSchool AI
Configure MagicSchool AI with clear prompts and rubrics to score writing and give specific, actionable suggestions. The quality comes from strong setup.
Design Animated Flashcards Canva
In Canva, create a slide per vocabulary word or concept, then add text, images, and animations using “Create an Animation.” Great for verbs in action or concept demos.
Quote Something Meaningful Canva
Search Canva for a “quote template.” Students pick a meaningful line from a text or lesson and pair it with visuals and typography reflecting the tone or message.

💛 SEL SUPPORT 5
Break Tasks into Manageable Steps Goblin.tools
For PBL or big assignments, use Compiler to break large projects into smaller, trackable steps so students can manage time, reduce stress, and see progress.
Build a Gratitude Wall Padlet
Launch a classwide or private Padlet using Gratitude Wall templates. Students post short messages, images, or GIFs to show appreciation and notice the good.
Ask “How Are We Feeling?” Canva
Use Canva’s ready‑to‑use “How Are We Feeling” slides for daily emotional check‑ins—project to the class or share for private responses.
Quiet and Calm the Room Classroom Zen
Try Typing Zen when energy runs high. Students practice typing encouraging phrases—calming, skill‑building, and a mindful movement break.
Build Conversational Confidence Magic School
Use the Language Practice Room template for ELLs to explore conversation simulations, practice comprehension, and record spoken responses in a low‑stakes space.

🎯 REFLECTION & EXIT TICKETS 4
Be the Bot AI Literacy
From Matt Miller (Ditch That Textbook): ask students to predict what an AI will answer to a lesson‑related question—identify vocabulary/concepts that should appear and what the AI might miss.
Set the Mood(board) Canva
In a shared Canva deck, give each student a slide to capture images, colors, and text that represent what they learned and how it felt—then gallery‑walk the deck.
Reverse‑Engineer an Exit Ticket Padlet / LMS
Instead of answering your question, students write their own and post it to a Padlet or LMS board. Turn the checkout into a quick peer‑learning moment with your probing replies.
Five‑Minute Reflection Canva
Have students grab a simple infographic template in Canva and fill it with key ideas, icons, and captions that capture the heart of the lesson in five minutes.
