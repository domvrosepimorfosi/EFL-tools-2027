What Is MagicSchool AI? Overview

MagicSchool AI is a collection of educator‑focused AI tools that help you plan, differentiate, create resources, and communicate more efficiently. Think of it as a co‑planner that drafts first passes so you can refine with your expertise.

  • Best for: drafting lesson materials, differentiating texts, brainstorming activities, generating rubrics, feedback, and emails.
  • Not for: grading high‑stakes work automatically, replacing teacher judgment, or handling student personal data.
Workflow at a Glance Big Picture
  1. Plan: unit goals → lesson outline → materials & differentiation.
  2. Teach: slides, discussion questions, station tasks, exemplars.
  3. Assess: quick checks, rubrics, feedback comments, retake versions.
  4. Communicate: family emails, progress reports, student reflections.
Inputs That Get Better Outputs Prompting

Give context (grade, subject, standards), constraints (time, language, tone), and examples (your rubric or a student sample). Use the templates in the Prompts section.

File Types & Formats Resources
  • Paste text or upload materials (if available in your plan). Use short excerpts for long texts.
  • When differentiating, specify target CEFR/reading level (e.g., B1 or Grade 6 readability) and vocabulary limits.
Time‑Saver Mindset Teaching Load

Use MagicSchool for first drafts of repetitive tasks. Spend your time on feedback, relationships, and tailoring to your class in Greece’s school context (e.g., phone‑ban, bilingual needs).

Accessibility & Language ELL

Ask for bilingual outputs (EN/EL) and plain‑language versions. Include audio or step‑by‑step formats to support diverse learners.

🛡️ SAFETY, PRIVACY & GOOD PRACTICE 6
No Student PII Privacy

Don’t enter full names, IDs, contact details, or sensitive info. Use initials or anonymized samples when modeling work.

Human‑in‑the‑Loop Professional Judgment

Treat outputs as drafts. Edit for local curriculum, cultural nuances, and appropriateness. Verify facts and answer keys.

Bias & Representation Checks Equity

Ask explicitly for diverse names, examples, and perspectives. Review for stereotypes and adjust accordingly.

Device‑Light Options Greece Policy

Request printable handouts and paper‑first workflows to align with the mobile phone ban in Greek schools.

Citations & Sources Transparency

When content draws on sources, ask MagicSchool to include citations or links and to flag uncertain claims.

Accessibility & UDL Inclusive

Request alt text for images, dyslexia‑friendly versions, low‑reading alternatives, and audio scripts.

❓ FAQ & TROUBLESHOOTING 5
It’s too generic. Quality

Add your exact objective, time, class profile, and an example of what good looks like. See the 5‑part prompt formula.

It hallucinated a fact. Accuracy

Ask for sources and verification steps. Provide your own text as the single source to reduce drift.

Tone is off. Voice

Specify: “warm but professional,” “student‑friendly B1,” or “formal memo.” Paste a sample paragraph to imitate.

Output is too long. Constraints

Set word/time limits, ask for bullet points, and request a printable one‑pager version.

I need it bilingual. EN/EL

Ask for parallel English/Greek outputs and include a glossary box (EN→EL) for key terms.
